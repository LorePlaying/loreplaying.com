Player steps in detail

  1. Choose a story and find a Lore Master. Finding other Players is optional.
  2. Print the Player Character Sheet and prepare for the pre-session.
  3. Discuss your character, role, and other details with the Lore Master.
  4. Take notes and set the session place and date.
  5. Join the session on time and enjoy your role-play!

Lore Master steps in detail

  1. Choose a story and gather the Players.
  2. Print the Lore Master Guide and prepare for the pre-sessions.
  3. Hold a pre-session with each Player.
  4. Discuss characters, roles, and other details and take notes.
  5. Set the session place and date with the Players.
  6. Prepare the Lore Play session.
  7. Manage the session while the Players play their roles.
  8. Take session notes during or after the session, and enjoy your LM experience!
