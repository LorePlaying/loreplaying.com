A Lore Play session brings together Players and a Lore Master to prepare, play, and develop a story.

    LM->>Player: Confirm the session
    Player->>Story: Play the role

    Note over Player,Story: The Lore Play session begins

## For Players

A person who wants to become part of a story:

1. **Roll a die** for their character in the story.
2. **Join a short pre-session** with the Lore Master.
3. **Prepare to play** their role in the session.

## For Lore Masters

A person who wants to become a Lore Master:

1. **Choose the Lore Play format** — One-shot, Campaign, Episodic, or another format.
2. **Hold a short pre-session meeting** with each player.
3. **Take notes in the Lore Play Zine (LPZ)** for each pre-session.
4. **Set the session date** and prepare to manage the session.
